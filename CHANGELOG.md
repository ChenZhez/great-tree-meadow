# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10

First public release.

- Explore 27 areas around the great tree and collect the twelve glow seeds.
- Companion actions: pet, treats, fetch, carry, dance, shake, photos.
- Home with extensions, 47 kinds of furniture, cooking and a vegetable patch.
- Fishing, bug catching, shop, field guide, achievements and daily tasks.
- English, Chinese and Japanese.
- Touch controls, comfort and accessibility settings, automatic quality.
- Runs from a single HTML file, online or offline.
